| Feature                         | Calculation                       | What it tells you                            |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Peak temperature (T_{\max})** | (\max(T))                         | Maximum thermal stress reached               |
| **Temperature rise (\Delta T)** | (T_{\max}-T_0)                    | Overall heating caused by degradation        |
| **Maximum thermal gradient**    | (\max(dT/dt))                     | How rapidly the inverter heats               |
| **Rise time**                   | Time from 10% → 90% of final rise | Whether degradation changes thermal dynamics |
| **Final / steady temperature**  | (T(t_{\rm end})) or steady mean   | Long-term thermal operating point            |
| **AUC above baseline**          | (\int (T-T_0),dt)                 | Total accumulated thermal exposure           |
